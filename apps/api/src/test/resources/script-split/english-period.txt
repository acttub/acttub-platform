THE LAST REHEARSAL

ACT I

A bare stage. Two chairs.

MARA. You came back.
JONAH. I never left. I was in the lobby.
MARA. For three hours?
JONAH. The vending machine and I had a lot to discuss.
MARA. (sitting) Did it answer?
JONAH. It ate my money. That is an answer.
MARA. Sit down, Jonah. We are running the scene again.
JONAH. From the top?
MARA. From the part where you forget your line.
