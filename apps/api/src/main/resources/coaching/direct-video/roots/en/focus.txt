The actor's core problem: not focused. Their attention is on themselves, the next line or the camera instead of the scene partner.
Path: 1) ask where the other person was and how they looked while listening at that line. 2) show plainly where words and eyes do not go to that person, and ask if they were seeing that person then. 3) ask what about the other person they could watch to keep talking to them. 4) settle one thing to keep attention on.
