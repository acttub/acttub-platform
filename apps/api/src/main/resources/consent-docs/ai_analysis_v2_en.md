# Consent to AI Analysis and Processing of Facial/Emotion Information

Effective Date: The date this version is published (shown on the app's consent screen and in the consent list in Settings)

This consent is a separate consent to the processing of videos, recordings, and scripts uploaded by the user, and the **facial expression, emotion, and other information** generated therefrom, for the AI acting analysis and coaching provided by Acttub (hereinafter the "Service"). This consent is required to provide the Service's core functionality, and if you do not consent, you will not be able to use the AI analysis feature.

## 1. Information Processed
- Acting **videos and audio** uploaded by the user
- **Script and line text** entered or selected by the user, and **line-by-line recordings** from script reading
- Conditions selected and requests written in "Create a Script Together", and text written to the AI coach
- **Analysis data such as the user's facial expression, emotional state, and spoken content**, extracted from videos and recordings
- Acting analysis results, reports, and coaching conversation content generated from the above data

## 2. Purpose of Processing
- Analysis of facial expression, emotion, and speech regarding the user's acting, and provision of objective feedback
- Acting and reading coaching based on the script, and creation of lines to practice
- Generation of analysis reports and AI coach follow-up conversations
- Analytical processing to the extent necessary to achieve the above purposes

## 3. Method of Processing and Use of Third Parties (AI Processing)
- The Service may transmit uploaded videos, images, and recordings, and **script and line text**, to **Google's generative AI (Gemini, etc.) and cloud infrastructure** for processing for AI analysis and coaching.
- Storage uses cloud storage such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), and data may be **transferred to servers outside Korea** in the course of processing.
- The specific entrustment and cross-border transfer status follows the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Retention and Destruction
- Videos used in analysis and their results are destroyed without delay when the user deletes them within the Service or withdraws membership.
- Users may delete individual videos or analysis results at any time.

## 5. Right to Refuse Consent and Disadvantages of Refusal
- Users have the right to refuse this consent.
- However, since facial expression and emotion analysis is a core feature of the Service, if you do not consent, you will not be able to use the video upload, analysis, report, and coaching features.

## 6. Withdrawal of Consent
- Users may withdraw this consent at any time through the in-Service settings or at acttub0527@gmail.com.
- Upon withdrawal, further AI analysis processing will stop, and related data will be destroyed in accordance with the Privacy Policy.
