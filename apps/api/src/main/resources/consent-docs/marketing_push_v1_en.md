# Consent to Receive Advertising Information (App Push, Optional)

Effective Date: The date this version is published (shown on the consent screen and in the consent list in Settings)

This consent is **optional**. Without it, you can still use every feature of the service. You just won't receive the news below as app push notifications.

## 1. What You Are Consenting To
You agree that Acttub may send you **advertising information for commercial purposes as app push notifications** under Article 50 of the Korean Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection, and may use your device's push token to do so.

This is different from allowing notifications in your phone settings. Allowing notifications lets your device show them; this consent decides whether you receive advertising notifications among them. Service notifications such as practice reminders, analysis results, and reactions to your videos follow the notification options in Settings regardless of this consent.

Advertising information sent by email is decided separately under "Consent to Receive Advertising Information (Email)".

## 2. What We Send
- New features and update news
- New scripts and official line challenges
- Event announcements

## 3. Personal Information Used
- Item: your device's push token
- Purpose: sending the advertising information in Section 2
- Retention: until you withdraw this consent or leave the service

## 4. How We Send
- The notification starts with "(Ad)" and states how to opt out. The sender is shown as the app name (Acttub), and the contact in Section 8 serves as the contact details.
- Tapping the notification lets you go straight to a screen where you can opt out.
- We do not send between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. the next day.
- When you agree or decline, we let you know the result.
- Every two years from the date you agreed, we ask whether you still want to receive it.

## 5. Changing Your Consent
You can turn it on or off at any time in the consent list in Settings. Changes are saved immediately.

## 6. Notices Sent Regardless of This Consent
Notices that affect your rights as a member, such as changes to the Terms or Privacy Policy and service maintenance or suspension, and service notifications are not advertising information and may be sent regardless of this consent.

## 7. Your Right to Decline
You may decline this consent, and declining has no effect on your use of the service.
