Effective Date: The date this version is published (shown on the consent screen and in the consent list in Settings)

## 1. What You Are Consenting To
You agree that Acttub may send you **advertising information for commercial purposes by email** under Article 50 of the Korean Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection, and may use your email address to do so.

Advertising information sent as app push notifications is decided separately under "Consent to Receive Advertising Information (App Push)".

## 2. What We Send
- New features and update news (newsletter)
- New scripts and official line challenges
- Event announcements

## 3. Personal Information Used
- Item: the email address we received from your login provider (Google, Apple, Kakao, Naver) when you signed up
- Purpose: sending the advertising information in Section 2
- Retention: until you withdraw this consent or leave the service

## 4. How We Send
- The subject starts with "(Ad)", and the body states the sender (Acttub), contact details, and how to opt out.
- We do not send between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. the next day.
- When you agree or decline, we let you know the result.
- Every two years from the date you agreed, we ask whether you still want to receive it.

## 5. Changing Your Consent
- You can turn it on or off at any time in the consent list in Settings. Changes are saved immediately.
- You can also opt out using the method written in any email we send.

## 6. Notices Sent Regardless of This Consent
Notices that affect your rights as a member, such as changes to the Terms or Privacy Policy and service maintenance or suspension, and replies to your inquiries are not advertising information and may be sent regardless of this consent.

## 7. Your Right to Decline
You may decline this consent, and declining has no effect on your use of the service.
