# Consent to Retention and Use of Video/Recordings After Withdrawal (Optional)

Effective Date: The date this version is published (shown on the app's consent screen and in the consent list in Settings)

This consent is **optional**. Even without consenting, the user can use every feature of the Service exactly the same.

## 1. What You Are Consenting To
When a user withdraws membership, **the default is to destroy without delay** the acting videos and reading recordings the user uploaded. This is because faces and voices cannot be rendered unidentifiable.

Only if the user consents to this document will the videos and recordings continue to be retained and used after withdrawal, for the purposes and period set out below.

## 2. Purposes of Retention and Use
- Service improvement — used to review and improve the quality of analysis and coaching.
- AI model training — used to train and evaluate the AI models used for acting analysis and coaching.

The data is not used for any purpose beyond the two above, and is not disclosed to outside parties.

## 3. Retention Period
**3 years** from the date of withdrawal. The data is destroyed once the period ends. Retaining it longer requires obtaining consent again.

## 4. How to Change Your Consent
- **While a member**: The user can change between consenting and declining at any time in the consent list in Settings. The change is saved immediately, and whether the data is destroyed is determined by the decision in effect at the time of withdrawal. If the user withdraws without having made a decision, it is treated as a decline and the data is destroyed.
- **After withdrawal**: The user may request withdrawal of consent through the contact information listed in the Privacy Policy. After verifying the user's identity by matching against the account previously logged in with the same login method (Google, Apple, Kakao, or Naver), the retained videos and recordings are destroyed.

## 5. Right to Refuse Consent
Users may refuse this consent, and there is no disadvantage of any kind for refusing.
