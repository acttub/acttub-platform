# Acttub Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-08-11 (Previous Effective Date: 2026-07-29)

Acttub (hereinafter the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) and other applicable laws, and processes users' personal information as follows.

## 1. Items of Personal Information Collected
The Company collects the following information to provide the Service.

**(1) Membership Registration and Authentication (Social Login)**
- Email address, social login identifier (the account identifier value provided by Google, Apple, etc.)
- Name or stage name entered by the user

**(2) Information Generated or Collected in the Course of Using the Service**
- Acting **videos and audio** uploaded by the user
- Practice information entered by the user, such as scene, character, and line (subtext) information
- **Facial expression and emotion analysis data and acting analysis results** extracted or generated from videos
- Service usage records, access logs, device information, usage statistics
- **Cookie-based identifiers and visit records** for analyzing use of the web service (screens visited, referral path, browser/device type) — see Section 6 below for details and how to opt out
- **Action records** of actions performed within the Service (which features were used, in what order, whether each step succeeded or failed, and the time), and **screen recordings** that preserve the usage screen in a replayable form — see Section 6 below for details and how to opt out
- **Error records** automatically logged when an error occurs in the Service (the error content and location, the address of the screen where the error occurred, browser/OS/device type, and time of occurrence) — see Section 7 below for details

**(3) Notice Regarding Facial Feature Information**
- The Company analyzes the **user's facial expression and emotional state** in videos for the purpose of providing acting feedback. The expression/emotion analysis data generated in this process is treated as potentially sensitive information and is processed only for the purpose of providing the Service.
- Specific processing details and the separate consent for this are set forth in the "AI Analysis Consent."

## 2. Purposes of Collection and Use of Personal Information
- Member identification and authentication, and provision of the Service
- AI-based facial expression, emotion, and acting analysis of acting videos, and provision of the results
- Service improvement, response to errors and fraudulent use, and handling of customer inquiries
- Service improvement through analysis of web service usage and referral paths
- Analysis of usage behavior and review of screen recordings to identify where users experience difficulty in the Service, and resulting feature improvements
- Detection, root-cause analysis, and recovery of Service errors
- Fulfillment of obligations under applicable law

## 3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information
1. The Company destroys personal information and uploaded Content without delay once the purpose of use is achieved or the user withdraws membership.
2. However, where retention is required under applicable law, the information is retained for the relevant period.
   - The period prescribed by applicable law for records relating to contracts, withdrawal of subscription, and the like
3. Users may delete individual videos and analysis results within the Service at any time, and upon deletion they are removed from storage.
4. The web usage analytics records and action records referenced in Section 6 are retained for up to 14 months from the date of collection, after which they are deleted.
5. The error records referenced in Section 7 are retained for up to 30 days from the date of collection, after which they are deleted.

## 4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties
As a general rule, the Company does not provide users' personal information to outside parties. However, the Company may provide it where there is a legal basis or the user's consent.

## 5. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing
The Company entrusts the processing of personal information as follows to provide the Service.

| Recipient | Entrusted Task |
| --- | --- |
| Google (Google Cloud / Gemini) | AI analysis processing of videos/images |
| Google (Google Analytics) | Web service usage statistics and referral path analysis |
| Amazon Web Services (AWS) | Cloud storage of videos and data |
| Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) | Collection and analysis of Service error records |
| Amplitude, Inc. | Collection and analysis of Service usage behavior (action records) and screen recordings |

- When entrusting processing, the Company reflects the relevant matters in its contracts to ensure the recipient processes personal information securely, and manages and supervises the recipient accordingly.
- If a recipient or an entrusted task changes, the Company will disclose this through this Policy.

## 6. Operation of and Opt-Out from Cookies and Other Automatic Collection Devices
The Company uses automatic collection devices on the web service to aggregate the following two categories, in the course of which a cookie or an equivalent identifier is stored in the user's browser.

**(1) Referral Path and Screen Usage Statistics — Google Analytics**
- Items collected: the address of the screen visited, referral path (the immediately preceding site/campaign information), browser/OS/device type, approximate region, and time of visit.
- How to opt out: Users may refuse cookie storage in their browser settings, or install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) provided by Google.

**(2) In-Service Action Records and Screen Recordings — Amplitude**
- The Company uses **Amplitude** to understand at which stage of practice users stop and which features are actually used, in order to fix and improve the Service.
- Items collected:
  - The type and time of actions performed (e.g., uploading a video, starting a Q&A conversation, viewing a practice note), whether each step succeeded or failed, and the failure reason code
  - The address of the screen visited (including values contained in the address, such as a practice session identifier), and the names of buttons/items clicked
  - Browser/OS/device type, time of access
  - **Screen recording (session replay)**: records the screen layout and operations during the user's use of the Service in a replayable form.
- **Notice Regarding Screen Recording**: Screen recordings capture the screen exactly as the user was viewing it, and this may include the **scene/character/goal information entered by the user, conversations exchanged with the coach, generated practice notes, and the practice video screen being played back**. The Company uses this record only for the purpose of finding and fixing errors and usability issues in the Service.
- How to opt out: Amplitude does not provide a separate blocking add-on. Users may refuse cookie/site data storage in their browser settings, or opt out by requesting suspension of processing under Section 9 below.

**(1) What Is Not Collected via the Google Analytics Path**
- **Name, email, uploaded videos, and practice content are not collected through this path.** Where an address contains a value such as the user's practice session identifier, the Company removes it before transmission.

**Common to (1) and (2)**
- **These cookies and identifiers are stored only after the user has consented to this Policy.** They are not stored before consent.
- **Name and email are not transmitted through either path.** Only the Company's internal member identification number is used to distinguish users.
- Opting out does not restrict use of the Service in any way.

## 7. Collection of Error Records
- The Company uses **Sentry** to detect and fix errors when they occur in the Service. Records are transmitted only at the moment an error occurs; ordinary usage behavior is not collected.
- Items collected: the error content and location, the address of the screen where the error occurred, browser/OS/device type, and time of occurrence.
- **Name, email, uploaded videos, practice content, and screen recordings are not collected through this path.** Where an address contains a value such as the user's practice session identifier, the Company removes it before transmission.
- No cookies are used in this path.

## 8. Cross-Border Transfer
Personal information may be transferred to servers outside Korea (e.g., overseas regions operated by an entrusted party) for AI analysis, cloud storage, web usage analysis, usage behavior analysis, and error record analysis. The items transferred, destination countries, timing, method, and retention period follow the status of entrustment described above.

## 9. User Rights and How to Exercise Them
Users may exercise the following rights at any time:
- The right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of their personal information
- Withdrawal of consent and withdrawal of membership
- The above rights may be exercised through in-service settings or via acttub0527@gmail.com.

## 10. Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information
- Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery, and printed materials are shredded or incinerated.
- Personal information for which grounds for destruction have arisen is destroyed without delay.

## 11. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information
The Company takes the security measures required by applicable law, including encryption during transmission and storage, control of access authority, and management of access records.

## 12. Children Under 14
The Service does not permit registration by children under 14 years of age, and does not collect personal information from such children.
