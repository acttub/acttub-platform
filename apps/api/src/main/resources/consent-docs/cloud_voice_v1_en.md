# Consent to Premium Reading Voice (Optional)

Effective Date: The date this version is published (shown when turning on the premium voice and in the consent list in Settings)

This consent is **optional**. Without it, script reading works exactly the same with the on-device voice.

## 1. What You Are Consenting To
When you turn on the "premium voice" in script reading, the **partner's line** is sent through our server to Google's speech synthesis service (Gemini) so it can be read in a more natural voice. If you do not turn it on, lines never leave your device and are voiced on the device.

## 2. What Is Sent
- One partner line and the selected voice type

We do not send the script title, character names, or anything that identifies you, such as your name or email. Your own lines and recordings are not sent.

## 3. Purpose
To turn the partner's lines into speech and play them during reading. Nothing else.

## 4. Processor and Overseas Transfer
- Processor: Google (Gemini)
- Destination: overseas servers operated by Google
- When and how: each time a partner line is read with the premium voice on, over an encrypted connection
- See the Privacy Policy for details on processors and overseas transfers.

## 5. Retention
- Generated audio is stored so the same line is not generated twice. It is looked up only by the line and voice type and is **not linked to who requested it.**
- Per user, we keep only the number of lines used per day, and delete it when you withdraw.

## 6. Changing Your Decision
You can withdraw this consent anytime in the consent list in Settings. From that moment, lines are no longer sent and the on-device voice is used.

## 7. Right to Decline
You may decline this consent without any disadvantage to any feature, including script reading.
