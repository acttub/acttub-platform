# Consent to Sharing Video Data with Third Parties (Optional)

This consent is **optional**. Even without consenting, the user can use every feature of the Service exactly the same.

## 1. What You Are Consenting To
You are consenting to the Company providing the acting videos and reading recordings you uploaded to the app to other companies or research institutes that build AI. The Company **may provide this data for a fee (sell it).** The videos and recordings contain your face and voice as they are, and faces and voices cannot be rendered unidentifiable.

## 2. Recipients
Companies and research institutes that develop or research AI models. Once a recipient is decided, the Company will add its name to this document, publish it as a new version, and ask for your consent again before providing any data. Only the data of users who consent to the new version will be provided to that recipient.

## 3. Recipients' Purposes of Use
Training, evaluation, and research of AI models, such as video and speech recognition and facial expression and acting analysis. The Company will require by contract that recipients do not use the data outside these purposes or pass it on to anyone else.

## 4. Items Provided
- Acting videos (including face, voice, facial expressions, and movement) and reading recordings
- Descriptions of the practiced scenes and the type of practice
- Gender and age group (in 10-year bands)

Your name, email address, login information, contact details, and payment information are not provided.

## 5. Recipients' Retention Period
**3 years** from the date the data is received. The Company will require by contract that the data be destroyed once the period ends.

## 6. How to Change Your Consent
- You can change between consenting and declining at any time in the consent list in Settings. The change is saved immediately.
- If you change to decline, no new data will be provided from then on, and the Company will ask recipients to destroy any data already provided.
- If you withdraw your membership or delete a practice in the app, its videos and recordings will no longer be provided.

## 7. Right to Refuse Consent
Users may refuse this consent, and there is no disadvantage of any kind for refusing.
